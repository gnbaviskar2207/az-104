# AZ-104 mistake journal

Use one entry for every missed, guessed, or slow question and every lab failure whose root cause was not immediately clear. Duplicate the blank entry; do not delete old attempts.

## Journal rules

- Record guessed correct answers as mistakes; they reveal an unstable decision.
- Quote only the short requirement phrase that changed the answer, not copyrighted practice questions.
- Explain why every selected option was wrong, not only why the correct one was right.
- Add Portal and CLI evidence that proves the Azure behavior.
- Schedule retests at 1, 3, 7, and 14 days until two correct closed-reference attempts.
- Tag the official domain/objective so weaknesses can be counted.

## Exam-readiness interpretation

- A falling mistake count is useful only if questions are new or sufficiently spaced.
- Repeated mistakes in one objective keep that objective Amber/Red in the [readiness tracker](objective-readiness.md).
- A high score with many guesses is not readiness.
- Close an entry only after evidence and two spaced correct attempts, not after reading the explanation once.
