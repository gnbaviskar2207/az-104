# AZ-104 assessment track

These original assessments follow the Microsoft AZ-104 skills measured as of April 17, 2026. They are learning aids, not exam dumps, and no question is claimed to appear on the live exam.

## Recommended sequence

Complete a guide module, close the guide, and take its checkpoint before opening the answer book:

1. [Environment and administrator workflow checkpoint](module-00-environment.md)
2. [Identity and governance checkpoint](module-01-identity-governance.md)
3. [Storage checkpoint](module-02-storage.md)
4. [Virtual networking checkpoint](module-03-networking.md)
5. [Compute, apps, and containers checkpoint](module-04-compute-apps-containers.md)
6. [Monitoring and recovery checkpoint](module-05-monitoring-recovery.md)
7. [Final Mock Exam 1](final-mock-01.md)
8. [Final Mock Exam 2](final-mock-02.md)

Answer books are under [answers](answers/README.md). Keep that directory closed until the timer ends.

## How to take a checkpoint

- Allow 25 minutes for each 15-question module checkpoint.
- Unless a question says **Choose two**, select one answer.
- For **Choose two**, award the point only when both choices are correct and no incorrect choice is selected.
- Target 12/15 (80%) before proceeding. At 10–11, review weak objectives and retake after 24 hours. At 9 or below, repeat the mapped labs before retaking.
- For every miss, write why your choice was wrong, why the correct choice satisfies the requirement, and the Portal/CLI action that proves it.
- Use a clean copy or record only question numbers and choices so a retake is not driven by remembered markings.

## How to take a final mock

- Each final mock has 50 questions and a 100-minute limit, matching the current AZ-104 assessment time and Microsoft's typical 40–60-question range.
- Treat each question as one point. Use 35/50 (70%) only as a practice baseline; Microsoft reports scaled scores and requires 700 or greater, so this raw score is not an official score conversion.
- Strong readiness target: at least 40/50 overall and at least 70% in every domain on two different attempts.
- Do Mock 1 after the capstone. Remediate by objective, wait at least 48 hours, then do Mock 2 under closed-book conditions.
- Microsoft Learn access exists during eligible role-based exams, but the timer continues. Take the first attempt closed-book; on a later attempt, practice finding only one or two uncertain facts quickly.

## Blueprint used for the final mocks

Each 50-question mock contains:

- 12 identity and governance questions (24%)
- 9 storage questions (18%)
- 12 compute questions (24%)
- 10 networking questions (20%)
- 7 monitoring and maintenance questions (14%)

This distribution stays within all five current objective ranges. Question styles include single choice, multiple response, ordered actions, short case studies, and Azure CLI interpretation. Markdown cannot reproduce the live exam interface, so use Microsoft's official Exam Sandbox separately.

## Integrity and currency

The authoritative scope is the [official AZ-104 study guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/az-104). Microsoft changes objectives and services over time. Check the study guide again two weeks before the exam and update any question whose premise no longer matches generally available behavior.
